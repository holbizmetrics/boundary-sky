The app's Moon vs the real Moon

The Moon in this sky follows a perfect ellipse that never changes: same shape, same tilt, forever. The real Moon is also pulled by the Sun, so its orbit stretches, wobbles and turns.

On 1 January 2000 both Moons start at the same place in the sky. The app's ellipse uses orbital elements valid for exactly that date, so this is its best day.
