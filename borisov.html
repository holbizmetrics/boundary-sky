2I/Borisov: two predictions, one withheld observation

In 2019 astronomers found a comet coming from another star. While it was still being tracked, they published two orbit predictions, a few weeks apart. Both pass through the early sightings (white dots). Each one says where the comet will be in March 2020.

Which prediction do you trust? You will not see who made them, when, or with how much data until after you choose.
